★★★★★

Great trek with an excellent guide

Sameer helped us out at the last minute by organizing a shorter version of the Kokhe Danda trek. Both he and the route exceeded our expectations.
As a guide, Sameer is excellent; he has a wealth of knowledge about local flora and fauna, can name and state the elevation of almost every mountain peak and shares insights into local culture and life in the mountain villages with great enthusiasm, willingness, and nuance. He also took some beautiful photos for us and even used his drone to provide a bird’s-eye view of the region. Sameer proved to be an attentive and highly helpful guide, without ever being overbearing. He was always ready to answer my countless questions, yet he also ensured we had plenty of privacy. A perfect balance.
The route itself is diverse and engaging. In our version of the trek, the first day began with a long climb up stairs through the forest, leading to a stunning viewpoint for the sunset right next to our guesthouse. The second day featured a ridge walk, offering more expansive, open vistas. We stayed in a small village where we learned how to make momos and took a stroll through the village, during which he explained the local crops and customs. On the third day, we headed back into the forest for our descent.
It was a truly wonderful experience, and I wouldn't hesitate to recommend it to anyone. Thanks, Sameer!
