Write the bansha

One line per person

Start with the oldest person. Indent two spaces for a child. Put + before a wife or husband, at the same indent. Mark (m) or (f). Dates go after a bar: | 1940-2012. Notes go after a second bar.

You do not type the numbers. The chart gives them: 1 the founder, 1.1 the first child, 1.2 the next, 1-s a spouse. Change the order, and the numbers follow.
