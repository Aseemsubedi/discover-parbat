Kathmandu to Dhap: How to Reach the Start of the Pikey Peak Trek

One long, early drive: 235 km from Chabahil via BP Highway and Okhaldhunga to Dhap, where Everest and Lhotse are already on the horizon. Here is how the jeep and bus work, what it costs and what the road is like.

The quick answer

Take a shared jeep heading for Salleri from the counter at Chabahil in Kathmandu, and get off at Dhap. Jeeps start picking up passengers around 5 am and aim to reach Dhap by about 2 pm.

The road is about 235 km and normally takes around 10 hours, going out along BP Highway and then up through Okhaldhunga. A jeep seat costs about NPR 2,000; the bus is a little cheaper. Dhap is where the Pikey Peak Trek begins, and on a clear day you can already see Everest and Lhotse from the village.

Jeep or bus to Dhap?

There is no separate service just for Dhap. Both jeeps and buses run from Kathmandu to Salleri, the headquarters of Solukhumbu district, and Dhap is a stop on the way, so tell the driver or the counter that you are getting off at Dhap. The counters for both are at Chabahil in Kathmandu.

We recommend the jeep. Jeeps start collecting passengers from around 5 am and set off aiming to reach Dhap by about 2 pm, which leaves you the afternoon to rest, walk around the village and watch the evening light on the mountains before the first trekking day. The bus costs a little less, around NPR 1,600, if you are watching your budget.

Our takeBook a jeep seat, be at Chabahil early, and treat the drive as part of the trip. You arrive with daylight to spare.

The route from Kathmandu to Dhap

The road leaves the Kathmandu Valley to the east and follows rivers for most of the day before it finally starts to climb. These are the main places you pass, in order.

  • Start · around 5 amChabahil, Kathmandu. Jeeps fill up and leave once passengers are collected.
  • BP HighwayOut past Dhulikhel and Panauti onto BP Highway, then along the banks of the Roshi Khola.
  • KhurkotThe road meets the Sunkoshi River and follows its banks.
  • GhurmiStill beside the Sunkoshi, in Udayapur.
  • HarkapurCross the Sunkoshi River. From here the road leaves the valley floor and starts gaining height.
  • OkhaldhungaThe climb continues through the hills of Okhaldhunga.
  • Dhap · around 2 pmGet off at Dhap. The jeep or bus carries on to Salleri.

Most of the drive, all the way to Harkapur, runs along the Roshi Khola and then the Sunkoshi. The change comes after Harkapur, when the road turns uphill and the air starts to cool. Dress in layers you can add as you climb.

What the road is like

Better than most people expect. The road is blacktopped and mostly two-lane almost the whole way. The one exception is the Roshi Khola section in Kavre and Udayapur, where the river swept the road away during a monsoon a couple of years ago. That stretch is still off-road, so expect a slow, bumpy section there.

In the monsoon: because so much of the route follows the Roshi and Sunkoshi rivers, this is the part of the drive most affected by heavy rain. If you are travelling between June and September, check road conditions the day before you go.

Arriving in Dhap

Dhap is a small roadside stop with teahouses, and it is where you spend the first night before walking. Arriving in the early afternoon means you can rest after the long drive, eat properly and get your gear sorted for the morning.

The best surprise is the view. On a clear day you can see Everest and Lhotse from Dhap itself, along with Numbur and Karyolung rising right behind the teahouses. It is a good taste of what is coming at the top of Pikey Peak (4,065 m).

From here the trek takes over. For the day-by-day route over Pikey Peak to Junbesi and Salleri, see the main Pikey Peak Trek page. If you are still choosing between this and a Khumbu trip, read Pikey Peak vs Everest View Trek.

Getting back to Kathmandu from Salleri

The Pikey Peak Trek finishes in Salleri, not Dhap, so you do not have to backtrack. Salleri is about 1.5 hours' drive further along the same road, which is why the return is a little longer: around 11.5 to 12 hours back to Kathmandu. A seat in a shared jeep from Salleri costs about NPR 2,500.

Tips for the drive

  • Be at Chabahil early. Jeeps start picking up passengers around 5 am.
  • Pack your own food. Highway food in Nepal is not great. Bring fruit and snacks for the day.
  • Carry water and a warm layer. It gets cooler once the road climbs after Harkapur.
  • Say "Dhap" when you book. Vehicles are going to Salleri, so make sure the driver knows where you are getting off.
  • No permits needed. The Pikey Peak Trek does not require any trekking permits, so there is nothing to arrange before you leave.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Dhap from Kathmandu?

About 235 km by road, via BP Highway, Khurkot, Ghurmi, Harkapur and Okhaldhunga. The drive normally takes around 10 hours.

Where do jeeps and buses to Dhap leave from in Kathmandu?

From the jeep and bus counters at Chabahil. They are heading for Salleri, and you get off at Dhap on the way. Jeeps start picking up passengers around 5 am and aim to reach Dhap by about 2 pm.

How much is the jeep from Kathmandu to Dhap?

As of November 2026, a seat in a shared jeep from Kathmandu to Dhap costs about NPR 2,000. The bus is around NPR 1,600. Fares change, so confirm at the counter in Chabahil.

Is the road to Dhap paved?

Mostly, yes. It is blacktopped and largely two-lane. The exception is the Roshi Khola section in Kavre and Udayapur, which is off-road since the river swept the road away in a monsoon a couple of years ago.

Can you see Everest from Dhap?

Yes. On a clear day you can see Everest and Lhotse from Dhap itself, before you even start walking to Pikey Peak.

How do I get back to Kathmandu after the Pikey Peak Trek?

The trek ends at Salleri, about 1.5 hours' drive beyond Dhap. Jeeps from Salleri to Kathmandu cost about NPR 2,500 and take around 11.5 to 12 hours.

Do I need a permit for the Pikey Peak Trek?

No. The Pikey Peak Trek does not need any trekking permits.
